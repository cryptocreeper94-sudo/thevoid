## 🌌 Welcome to THE VOID

**THE VOID** is a sanctuary for absolute emotional truth. Unlike conventional meditation apps that offer generic platitudes, THE VOID is a cryptographically isolated mental wellness platform built entirely on the Trust Layer ecosystem. 

It is designed to give you a secure, completely private environment to process extreme emotions, log your mental state, and interact with deterministic synthetic agents that range from deeply empathetic to unapologetically sarcastic. 

Because THE VOID utilizes the **Lume-V Deterministic Engine**, your emotional data is processed locally and securely—meaning there are zero cloud hallucinations, and zero corporate data harvesting.

### Core Features

* **The Rage Room & Vent Library:** Sometimes you just need to scream. Safely unload your extreme frustrations into the Rage Room. Your data is processed, analyzed for emotional release, and then securely locked away or destroyed in the Void Echo.
* **Voice Fingerprinting & Mood Analytics:** The engine analyzes the micro-tremors and tone in your voice as you speak, charting a highly accurate Mood Portrait over time.
* **Encrypted Voice Journaling:** Speak your mind into the encrypted journal. Your entries are secured by the TrustVault architecture, guaranteeing absolute privacy.
* **Synthetic Organism Personas:** Don't want to talk to a robot? Choose an agent that fits your mood. Whether you need a compassionate listener, a stoic philosopher, or a sarcastic cynic who tells it like it is, the Axiom agents adapt to you.
* **Crisis Toolkit & Emergency Helplines:** Immediate, hardcoded access to verified emergency resources and grounding exercises for when you need real-world intervention fast.

### Building Native Binaries Locally

**For Android (.apk)**
1. `npm install`
2. `npm run build`
3. `npx cap sync android`
4. Open the `android` folder in Android Studio and build the signed APK.

**For Windows (.exe)**
*(Electron Builder Configured)*
1. `npm run build`
2. `npx electron-builder --win`
3. The setup executable will be generated in the `dist` folder.
